**Rule in force since 2026-09-28:** "everything closes on its day at the time at which its
specific exchange closes". Every instrument's past close is Bloomberg's daily close for it on
that date (daily PX_LAST for FX spot, the FX tenor tickers, futures, listed options, LME
pillars), stamped 17:00 New York of its date: `pull_marks.CLOSE_HOUR_NY = 17`, the one close
stamp of the app, equal to `live.ROLLOVER_HOUR_NY`. Nothing reads an intraday bar. This
reversed the 15:00 New York FX close of 2026-09-21 / 09-22 (which the earlier note
[[eod-1500-futures-2026-09-28]] still described as in force: read that note as history).

**Why:** two conventions on one P&L line (FX at a 15:00 bar, futures at the 17:00 daily close)
were dropped for one daily close per instrument, the exchange's own.

**How to apply:**
- A live press's rows (spot, forwards, FUTURE_PX, LME) all carry the real press time
  (`_now_iso()`); `build_future_rows(..., snapped=)` exists for that, default
  `snapped_at(as_of)` = 17:00 for the backfill's calls. Never stamp a live row at 17:00: the
  backfill's `is_close_row` tells a past row from the close by that stamp alone.
- The probe step is `daily_close` (fetch_historical_series, EURUSD PX_LAST, the business day
  before --as-of); `intraday_close_bar` and the IntradayBarRequest fakes are gone from
  pull_marks and test_bloomberg.
- `INTRADAY_HISTORY_BUSINESS_DAYS` and `CLOSE_REASON` were kept in pull_marks only because
  backfill.py imports them at module level (bbg-backfill was rewriting in parallel); delete
  the first once backfill.py stops importing it.
- Out of this lane, left for the housekeeper: `tools/bloomberg_terminal_probe.py` (its own
  CLOSE_HOUR_NY = 15 and an IntradayBarRequest check), `tools/bbg_diagnostics.py` ("Past
  closes at 15:00 New York" row), `tests/test_auto_backfill.py` (monkeypatches
  `pm.fetch_intraday_close_series`, which raises AttributeError now), `fwd_curve.py` docstring.
- `tests/test_live.py::test_availability_no_blpapi_never_touches_the_socket` fails on this PC
  since blpapi got installed (environmental, pre-existing; the test assumes no blpapi).
