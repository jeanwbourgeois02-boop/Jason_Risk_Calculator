User yes 2026-09-28 (hard rule 7): "everything closes on its day at the time at which its specific exchange closes". The app's one close stamp is 17:00 New York of the row's own date (Bloomberg's daily close) for every mark; the 15:00 close of 2026-09-21 is gone.

**Why:** one convention for the futures book; bbg-live / bbg-backfill changed the Bloomberg side in the same wave.

**How to apply:**
- `store.close_stamp(day)` = 17:00 America/New_York, offset resolved, built from `store.CLOSE_HOUR_NY` locally. It used to delegate to rates-pricer's `engine.rates.store.snapped_at` (still 15:00 at the time; a Request to rates-pricer was filed). Do not re-import it: the store's stamp must not depend on another lane's constant.
- `cut_time_factor`'s default moment (no readable SPOT stamp) is also `CLOSE_HOUR_NY`, so a past close's FX PREMIUM and Greeks are priced two hours later than before (a tiny bit less time value on very short options).
- `live_stamp`, the payoff rule, the close-out rule and the expiry catch-up were untouched.
- Tests pin `T17:00:00-04:00` (test_options_close.py) and `sqrt((4 - 7/24)/4)` for the no-stamp cut-time case (test_options_pricing.py, my test in the shared file).
